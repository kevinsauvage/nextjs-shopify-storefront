How to measure

Measure over light clothing and keep the tape snug but not tight. If you fall between two sizes, we recommend sizing up — especially for outerwear.

Tops (cm)

Size Chest Waist Hips
XS 80–84 62–66 88–92
S 84–88 66–70 92–96
M 88–94 70–76 96–102
L 94–100 76–82 102–108
XL 100–107 82–89 108–115

Jewelry

  • Necklaces: 40 cm sits at the collarbone, 50 cm at the neckline.
  • Bracelets: add 1–2 cm to your wrist measurement for comfort.
  • Rings: measure in the evening, when fingers are slightly larger.

Still unsure? Contact us with your measurements and we will recommend a size before you order.
